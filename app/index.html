Take a note.

One tap gives you three real Marigold bearer notes — 10, 1 and 0.1 MAGLD, 11.10 in all. Testnet money: worthless by design, real in every other way.

These are bearer notes. Whoever holds these codes owns the money. Take them into your wallet now, then treat this screen as spent paper.

In your wallet, once it is connected: receive <code> for each note, the big one first. Paste the text or scan the QR from another screen. The wallet checks the note on the chain and makes it yours alone; from that moment nobody else can spend it.
